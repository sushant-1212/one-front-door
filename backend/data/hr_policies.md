# Contoso Enterprise Employee Policy Handbook 2026

## Section 1.0: Equal Employment & Workplace Standards
Contoso Enterprise is committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and collaborative work environment. Discrimination, harassment, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated. All employees are expected to uphold the Contoso Code of Business Conduct.

## Section 2.1: Annual Leave & PTO Accrual
Full-time regular employees accrue 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued semi-monthly at 1.67 days per pay period. Employees with over 3 years of service accrue 25 days per calendar year. A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may roll over into the subsequent fiscal year and must be used by March 31st.

## Section 2.2: Sick Leave & Medical Appointments
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year allocated on January 1st. Sick leave covers personal illness, medical appointments, and care for immediate family members. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days, a physician's note is required upon return.

## Section 3.1: Remote & Hybrid Work Guidelines
Employees eligible for hybrid work are expected to be in their designated regional office 2 to 3 days per week as agreed upon with their team manager. Core collaboration hours across all time zones are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time. Remote employees must maintain high-speed, secure internet access.

## Section 3.2: Home Office & Equipment Stipend
All full-time employees are eligible for a one-time home office setup stipend of up to $1,000 USD upon hire for ergonomic chairs, monitors, and desks. Additionally, employees receive an annual technology refresh stipend of $250 USD, claimable via Concur under Category: 'WHA-Stipend'.

## Section 4.1: Maternity Leave
Birthing parents are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave at 100% base salary. Maternity leave can commence up to 2 weeks prior to the expected delivery date. Contoso covers health insurance premiums during the entire leave duration.

## Section 4.2: Paternity & Caregiver Leave
Non-birthing parents, secondary caregivers, and adoptive parents are entitled to 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave at 100% base salary. This leave may be taken continuously or in two separate blocks of at least 2 weeks each within the first 12 months of the child's birth, adoption, or foster placement.

## Section 4.3: Bereavement & Compassionate Leave
Employees are granted up to 5 consecutive paid business days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, partner, parent, sibling, child). Up to 3 paid business days are granted for extended family members. Additional unpaid leave may be requested through HR.

## Section 5.1: Health, Dental & Wellness Reimbursement
Contoso provides comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance with 90% company-subsidized premiums. Additionally, employees receive an annual Wellness Credit of $600 USD per calendar year for gym memberships, fitness trackers, wellness apps, and mental health counseling.

## Section 5.2: Professional Development & Tuition Assistance
Employees who have completed 6 months of continuous service can receive up to $2,500 USD per calendar year for accredited certifications, relevant university coursework, and technical conferences, subject to manager approval.

## Section 6.1: Travel Allowance & Meal Per Diem
Business travel expenses must be booked via Navan or Concur. The standard domestic meal per diem is $75 USD per day ($20 breakfast, $25 lunch, $30 dinner) without requiring itemized receipts, though corporate card use is preferred. International per diems follow State Department standard rates.

## Section 7.1: Internal Job Transfers & Mobility
Employees in good standing who have spent at least 12 months in their current role are eligible to apply for internal job openings without notifying their manager until the second-round interview stage.
